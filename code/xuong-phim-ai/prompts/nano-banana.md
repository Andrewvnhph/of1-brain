# Nano Banana 2 — Character Reference Sheet Prompts

Dùng trong Google Flow → Nano Banana 2 (hoặc Imagen 3) để tạo Character Reference Sheet.
Generate **1 reference sheet per nhân vật** — sau đó dùng sheet này làm Character Reference
cho toàn bộ 24 shots Veo 3.1.

---

## Base Style (thêm vào đầu mọi prompt)

```
Studio Ghibli-inspired 2D hand-drawn animation, chibi proportions,
large round expressive head with big emotive eyes and small nose,
warm light tan skin, rosy cheeks, painterly soft coloring,
clean white background for reference sheet, full body visible,
consistent character design across all poses shown
```

---

## Nhân Vật 1: MINH (Student)

**Character Bible:**
- Tuổi: ~12, học sinh tiểu học/THCS
- Tóc: đen, rối nhẹ, hơi dựng lên (spiky-fluffy)
- Mắt: tròn to, nâu đậm, con ngươi tròn, hay bày tỏ cảm xúc
- Má: hồng nhẹ luôn hiện diện
- Trang phục: áo thun trắng ngắn tay, quần short xanh navy
- Tính cách: tò mò, hay tranh luận, biểu cảm mạnh

### PROMPT — Reference Sheet (1 ảnh, 6 poses)

```
Studio Ghibli-inspired 2D hand-drawn chibi character reference sheet for MINH,
Vietnamese boy age 12, large round expressive head with big dark brown round eyes,
small rosy cheeks, slightly spiky-fluffy dark black hair, warm light tan skin,
white short-sleeve t-shirt, dark navy shorts, simple sneakers.

Show 6 poses arranged in 2 rows of 3 on clean white background:
[Row 1]
1. Standing neutral — arms relaxed at sides, calm curious expression
2. Excited pointing — right arm extended pointing forward, mouth open in delight, eyes wide
3. Confused thinking — head tilted sideways, one hand on chin, eyebrows furrowed

[Row 2]
4. Surprised stunned — both arms dropped, eyes extremely wide, mouth open in O shape
5. Happy triumphant — both arms raised above head, big crescent smile, eyes curved
6. Skeptical crossed-arms — arms crossed over chest, frown, eyebrows angled down

Label each pose with its name below in small text.
Consistent character design, same proportions and costume across all 6 poses.
Painterly Ghibli soft coloring, warm tones, no background, white sheet.
```

## Nhân Vật 2: AN (Scientist)

**Character Bible:**
- Tuổi: ~35, nhà khoa học/giáo viên
- Tóc: đen, gọn, ngắn — kiểu người lớn chỉnh tề
- Mắt: hạnh nhân dài hơn Minh, bình tĩnh, thường hơi nheo nheo khi cười
- Má: ít hồng hơn Minh — trưởng thành hơn
- Trang phục: áo lab coat trắng, bên trong áo xanh nhạt
- Tính cách: bình tĩnh, kiên nhẫn, hay mỉm cười nhẹ

### PROMPT — Reference Sheet (1 ảnh, 6 poses)

```
Studio Ghibli-inspired 2D hand-drawn chibi character reference sheet for AN,
Vietnamese adult scientist age 35, large round head slightly smaller than child's,
calm gentle almond-shaped eyes, neat short dark hair combed neatly,
warm light tan skin, slight smile as default expression, white lab coat over light blue shirt.

Show 6 poses arranged in 2 rows of 3 on clean white background:
[Row 1]
1. Standing neutral — arms at sides, calm gentle smile, default expression
2. Explaining — one arm raised open palm toward viewer, knowing expression
3. Thinking — one finger raised upward, slight head tilt, curious eyebrow raise

[Row 2]
4. Holding object — both hands holding small glass prism prop at waist level, focused look
5. Gentle encouragement — one hand extended toward other person, warm reassuring smile
6. Eureka — one arm raised with index finger pointing up, eyes slightly wider than usual

Label each pose with its name below in small text.
Consistent character design, same proportions and lab coat across all 6 poses.
Painterly Ghibli soft coloring, warm tones, no background, white reference sheet.
Clearly distinguishable from Minh — adult proportions, calmer face, lab coat.
```

## Prompt — 2 Nhân Vật Cùng Frame (Two-Shot Reference)

Dùng để test character interaction và tỷ lệ chiều cao tương đối:

```
Studio Ghibli-inspired 2D hand-drawn character comparison reference sheet.
Two chibi characters standing side by side on white background.

LEFT — MINH: Vietnamese boy age 12, large round head, big dark brown expressive eyes,
spiky-fluffy black hair, rosy cheeks, white t-shirt, dark navy shorts.
Shorter in height, more energetic open posture.

RIGHT — AN: Vietnamese adult scientist age 35, slightly taller than Minh,
round head proportionally smaller ratio than child, calm almond eyes, neat dark hair,
white lab coat over light blue shirt, gentle default smile.

Both facing front, full body visible, clear height difference (An ~1.3x taller than Minh).
Painterly Ghibli soft coloring, warm light tan skin for both, white background.
Label LEFT as "MINH — Student" and RIGHT as "AN — Scientist".
```

## Checklist Trước Khi Dùng Làm Reference

- [ ] Minh và An phân biệt được rõ (tóc, trang phục, biểu cảm mặc định)
- [ ] Tỷ lệ chiều cao đúng: An cao hơn Minh ~30%
- [ ] 6 biểu cảm Minh đủ rõ — đặc biệt: surprised + triumphant + skeptical
- [ ] Lab coat An trắng, phân biệt với áo trắng Minh (bởi cổ áo + độ dài)
- [ ] Style nhất quán giữa các poses (cùng màu da, tóc, áo)
- [ ] Upload cả 3 sheets (Minh, An, Two-Shot) vào Google Flow trước khi generate Veo
