# Nano Banana 2 — Character Prompt Templates

Dùng trong Google Flow → Nano Banana 2 để tạo Character Reference Sheet.
Generate **6 poses per nhân vật** trước khi bắt đầu Veo 3.1.

---

## Base Style (thêm vào đầu mọi prompt)

```
Minimalist stick figure illustration, simple black line drawing on pure white background,
clean whiteboard style, 2-color accent palette: blue (#2563EB) and orange (#EA580C),
full body visible, no shading, no textures, no background elements,
flat vector-like appearance, educational animation style
```

---

## Nhân Vật 1: Scientist (An)

### Pose 1 — Standing Neutral
```
[BASE STYLE]
Scientist stick figure, standing upright, arms relaxed at sides,
small oval glasses outline on face, lab coat outline (simple rectangle shape),
neutral calm expression, centered in frame
```

### Pose 2 — Pointing at Whiteboard
```
[BASE STYLE]
Scientist stick figure with glasses, right arm extended forward pointing,
left arm bent at side, slightly forward-leaning posture,
orange accent dot on pointing finger tip, engaged expression
```

### Pose 3 — Thinking (hand on chin)
```
[BASE STYLE]
Scientist stick figure with glasses, right hand raised to chin level,
head tilted slightly left, contemplative posture,
left arm crossed at waist, blue accent on glasses outline
```

### Pose 4 — Excited / Eureka
```
[BASE STYLE]
Scientist stick figure with glasses, both arms raised above head in celebration,
slight jump implied by raised heels, excited open expression,
orange accent star or burst shape near head
```

### Pose 5 — Walking / Moving
```
[BASE STYLE]
Scientist stick figure with glasses, mid-stride walking pose,
left leg forward right leg back, arms naturally swinging,
carrying small rectangular object (book or prism) in one hand
```

### Pose 6 — Presenting / Explaining
```
[BASE STYLE]
Scientist stick figure with glasses, standing with one hand open toward viewer,
other hand holding pointer stick, teacher stance,
blue accent on pointer, slight forward lean toward viewer
```

## Nhân Vật 2: Student (Minh)

### Pose 1 — Standing Neutral
```
[BASE STYLE]
Student stick figure, slightly shorter than scientist figure, standing upright,
backpack outline on back (simple rectangle), curious open expression,
no glasses, hands at sides
```

### Pose 2 — Confused / Questioning
```
[BASE STYLE]
Student stick figure with backpack outline, head tilted sideways,
one hand raised with question mark shape above,
shoulders slightly raised, questioning expression,
orange question mark accent above head
```

### Pose 3 — Writing / Noting
```
[BASE STYLE]
Student stick figure, slightly crouched over, one arm extended holding pencil shape,
other arm supporting, focused downward gaze,
blue accent on pencil tip
```

### Pose 4 — Excited Discovery
```
[BASE STYLE]
Student stick figure, both arms out wide in surprise,
mouth open shape, body leaning back slightly,
blue and orange burst shapes radiating outward from figure
```

### Pose 5 — Watching / Observing
```
[BASE STYLE]
Student stick figure, arms crossed at chest, leaning slightly forward,
gaze directed to one side (toward experiment),
focused expression, slightly squinted eye shapes
```

### Pose 6 — Thumbs Up / Agreement
```
[BASE STYLE]
Student stick figure, right arm extended with thumb-up shape,
slight smile expression, standing straight,
orange accent on thumb shape
```

## Quality Checklist (trước khi approve)

- [ ] Style nhất quán giữa 6 poses (cùng line weight, cùng màu accent)
- [ ] Nhân vật rõ ràng ở kích thước 200×200px (sẽ nhỏ trong frame rộng)
- [ ] Background thuần trắng — không có shadow, gradient, texture
- [ ] Scientist và Student phân biệt được rõ (kính vs không kính, balo)
- [ ] Accent màu chỉ dùng blue + orange — không màu khác
